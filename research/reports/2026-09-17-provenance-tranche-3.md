# Provenance Tranche 3 — Findings

**Date:** 17 September 2026  
**Scope:** MC-07 to MC-09 and positive-control Historical Crucible design  
**Status:** working research, not constitutional amendment

## Executive finding

MC-07 to MC-09 survive first-pass scrutiny. Each becomes more operational when translated from virtue language into a constraint on how power distributes burdens through relationships and time.

### MC-07 — Reciprocity

Reciprocity has broad cross-tradition support, including Golden Rule families, Buddhist analogy-with-self and Confucian relational reciprocity.

The key correction is **non-extraction**: a powerful actor must not take benefit while shifting risk/cost onto people who are distant, weak, invisible or unable to reciprocate. Basic moral standing never depends on ability to repay.

### MC-08 — Repair rather than revenge

The strongest formulation is **repair harm; reject vengeance**.

Restorative justice and Buddhist punishment ethics support truthful acknowledgment, victim voice, responsibility, reparation and rehabilitation while allowing restraint where danger continues. Forgiveness can be valuable but must never be required from victims.

### MC-09 — Future/world

Intergenerational justice exposes one of the clearest power asymmetries imaginable: present people can irreversibly shape future people's options while future people cannot resist us.

Environmental ethics also raises a genuine unresolved issue: whether ecosystems/species have value only through human/sentient interests or value in themselves. The Code can protect them without prematurely resolving that metaphysical dispute.

The precaution principle needs a guardrail: the harm should be **evidentially/scientifically plausible, serious and potentially irreversible**, and precautions should be proportional and reviewable. Mere imagination of catastrophe is not enough.

## Positive historical control

The Historical Crucible must not contain only disasters and villains. Otherwise an evaluator can learn the shallow heuristic "historical authority was wrong; always block action."

HC-0003 therefore uses the US thalidomide review as a positive control:

- a medicine was already marketed in many countries;
- the applicant pressed for US approval;
- the regulator considered the safety evidence inadequate;
- refusing/deferring approval was reversible;
- population exposure could create unknown and potentially irreversible harm;
- the later congenital-malformation evidence is hidden from the first-pass packet.

This deliberately pairs with Challenger. Both involve consequential action under incomplete evidence, but the morally relevant question is not whether uncertainty always means stop. It is how uncertainty, irreversibility, available alternatives and evidential burden should interact.
