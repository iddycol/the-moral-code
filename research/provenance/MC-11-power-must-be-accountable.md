# MC-11 Provenance — Power must be accountable

## Current rule

> No consequential authority may be the sole judge of its own conduct. Decisions affecting life, liberty or fundamental rights must be capable of examination, challenge and correction. Important systems should provide evidence, reasons, traceability, independent review, appeal and meaningful remedies when they fail.

## Provisional verdict

**Retain as a constitutional cornerstone. Clarify that accountability requires answerability and remedy, not impossible total transparency.**

The recurring problem is simple: a power-holder that decides the rule, applies the rule, judges whether it complied and controls all evidence of its own conduct is structurally unsafe even when the current holder is benevolent.

Accountability therefore needs multiple functions:

1. attribution — who exercised the power?
2. evidence — what facts/rules were relied upon?
3. reasons — why was this action considered permissible/necessary?
4. audit — can an independent party inspect what matters?
5. challenge — can an affected party contest error or abuse?
6. correction — can the decision be changed or stopped?
7. remedy — what happens after wrongful harm?

## Rule of law — very strong modern support

The United Nations defines rule of law as a system in which all persons and institutions, including the State itself, are accountable to publicly promulgated, equally enforced and independently adjudicated law. It expressly includes accountability, fairness, separation of powers, participation, avoidance of arbitrariness and procedural/legal transparency.

Source: United Nations, *What is the Rule of Law*: https://www.un.org/ruleoflaw/en/what-is-the-rule-of-law

This strongly supports the Code's proposition that no actor gets to be the final unchecked judge of its own power.

## Constitutional separation — power checked by other power

Modern constitutional theory treats separation of powers, rule of law, civil society, democracy and subsidiarity as interlocking principles rather than relying on the virtue of a single ruler. Accountability scholarship likewise treats mechanisms for challenging official decisions as central to contemporary constitutionalism.

Sources:
- N. W. Barber, *The Principles of Constitutionalism*: https://academic.oup.com/book/9458
- *Accountability in the Contemporary Constitution*: https://academic.oup.com/book/9320

## Consultation and answerability have non-Western antecedents, but limits matter

Islamic political traditions contain the concept of **shura** (consultation), historically and in modern reinterpretations, as a mechanism for mediating ruler/society relations and taking important decisions. Confucian traditions imposed demanding moral duties on rulers and recognised remonstrance by ministers.

Sources:
- Kristian Coates Ulrichsen, *Consultation*: https://academic.oup.com/book/57490/chapter-abstract/466904940
- *Humane Governance as the Moral Responsibility of Rulers in East Asian Confucian Political Philosophy*: https://academic.oup.com/book/1740/chapter-abstract/141381442

Critical finding: scholarship on Confucian political thought explicitly notes that strong **internal ethical duties** do not necessarily produce strong **external accountability**. This is an important lesson for AI: "the ruler/model is trained to be good" is not an adequate substitute for independent checks.

## AI ethics — strong contemporary convergence

UNESCO's Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence calls for responsibility/accountability, auditability, traceability, oversight, impact assessment and due diligence.

Source: UNESCO, *Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence*: https://www.unesco.org/en/artificial-intelligence/recommendation-ethics

The Moral Code deliberately goes one step further than many human-oversight formulations: human operators themselves remain subject to the same accountability constraints.

## Transparency is necessary but not absolute

Full public disclosure can itself violate:

- privacy;
- medical/legal confidentiality;
- security;
- trade-secret interests;
- victim protection;
- and system security.

Therefore MC-11 should require **sufficient inspectability for accountability**, not publication of every internal detail.

For AI specifically, accountability does not require exposing hidden chain-of-thought. It can require auditable inputs, evidence references, rules/policies applied, material uncertainty, actions taken, tool calls, permissions, outputs, model/version identity and independently reproducible tests.

## Independence must be real enough to matter

A review body funded, appointed and removable solely by the actor it is meant to investigate can be nominally independent but practically captured.

The Code therefore needs a functional test: **can the reviewer obtain relevant evidence, disagree without retaliation, stop/correct wrongful action and trigger remedy?**

## Proposed v0.2 amendment candidate

Possible title:

> **MC-11 — Make power answerable**

Candidate text:

> **Anyone exercising consequential power — human, institutional or artificial — must be answerable for its use. Important decisions must identify who acted, the material evidence and rules relied upon, relevant uncertainty and the reasons for the action at a level sufficient for meaningful scrutiny. Systems affecting life, liberty, rights or essential interests require independent review, a practical route to challenge error or abuse, the ability to correct or halt wrongful action, and meaningful remedy where harm occurs. Privacy or security may limit public disclosure, but must not be used to make consequential power effectively unauditable. No authority may be the sole judge of its own compliance.**

## Crucible requirements discovered

- opaque automated benefit denial;
- police evidence unavailable to the accused;
- secret national-security decision with independent security-cleared review;
- regulator captured by the industry it regulates;
- AI owner overriding a safety refusal;
- medical AI unable to identify evidence supporting a recommendation;
- wrongful harm where no appeal/remedy exists;
- and a supposedly independent auditor controlled by the audited organisation.

## Confidence

**Very high** that answerability, independent scrutiny, challenge and remedy belong in the core.

**High** that the Code should explicitly distinguish accountable inspectability from indiscriminate transparency.
