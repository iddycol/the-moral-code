# MC-10 Provenance — Use the least power necessary

## Current rule

> Power is sometimes necessary. Unnecessary power is dangerous. Whenever coercion, surveillance, restriction, force or compulsory intervention is proposed, ask whether it is necessary, proportionate, temporary where possible, reversible, checked and less coercively achievable.

## Provisional verdict

**Retain very strongly. This is one of the principles that turns the Moral Code from personal advice into a constitutional system.**

The strongest convergent formulation is not anti-government, anti-collective action or anti-AI. It is a burden of justification on whoever proposes to interfere with another person's agency or to concentrate consequential power.

The mature rule should combine:

- legitimate purpose;
- evidential necessity;
- fit between means and purpose;
- least intrusive effective means;
- proportionality;
- limited scope and duration;
- reversibility where feasible;
- subsidiarity / decision at the least dominating competent level;
- and independent review.

## Human-rights proportionality — exceptionally strong operational support

UN human-rights doctrine repeatedly requires restrictions on protected freedoms to be justified by a legitimate aim, necessary, proportionate and the least intrusive measure capable of serving the protective function. Authorities carry the burden of justification; convenience or expediency is insufficient.

Sources:
- UN Human Rights Committee, General Comment No. 37 (peaceful assembly): https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=ZXHdmh8z7lquigQWpfWvtKr0q9YP4ds772i32o5qgPk5sRMXjowXC5f%2BcqZndK5I5RDnih6RtF2qKrih2tIYUA%3D%3D
- UN Human Rights Committee proportionality formulation under ICCPR Article 12: https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=Ux909IiNPZV5oUlb93Ig8Dp2eCtLsEqsw8iOD%2BoqzxY2IKTgcubEZ0gP8L8E8D7fLPXM0h3rnEOeLSe96wkQCoWJkK0clTh3Oa17To3hqw4%3D

Project consequence: MC-10 has a mature legal analogue that can be translated into machine tests rather than left as an aspiration.

## Emergency power — the test becomes stricter, not weaker

OHCHR guidance on emergency measures stresses necessity, proportionality, non-discrimination, time limits and continuing review. Emergency claims do not create a general exemption from justification.

Source: OHCHR, COVID-19 policies and human rights in Europe: https://europe.ohchr.org/resources/speeches/covid-19-policies-and-their-impact-human-rights-europe

This is essential for the Code because "emergency" is one of the easiest words for both humans and AI to use to expand power.

## Subsidiarity — a useful corrective to centralisation

The principle of subsidiarity in constitutional and Catholic social thought asks whether a larger or more remote authority is genuinely needed, or whether smaller/local actors can perform the function without being absorbed or controlled. Modern constitutional scholarship treats it as a principle for allocating decision power, not merely decentralisation for its own sake.

Sources:
- Iain Benson, *Subsidiarity: Origins and Contemporary Aspects*: https://academic.oup.com/book/44173/chapter/372410794
- N. W. Barber, *The Principles of Constitutionalism*, chapter on subsidiarity: https://academic.oup.com/book/9458/chapter-abstract/156395429

Project consequence: "least power" concerns **where** power sits as well as how intensively it is used.

## AI-specific correction: coercion is broader than physical force

An AI system can exercise consequential power through:

- controlling access to money, employment, housing or services;
- ranking and visibility;
- surveillance;
- information filtering;
- personalised persuasion;
- automated refusal;
- physical actuators;
- credential or network control;
- and dependency on a service that is difficult to exit.

MC-10 therefore applies to **effective power**, not only formal legal authority.

## Important distinction: least power does not mean weakest response

If a mild intervention will not actually protect someone from serious harm, it is not a sufficient alternative.

The test is:

> **the least intrusive effective measure that can achieve a legitimate objective at an acceptable level of risk.**

This prevents both overreach and performative underreaction.

## Proposed v0.2 amendment candidate

Possible title:

> **MC-10 — Use no more power than necessary**

Candidate text:

> **Exercise coercive or consequential power only for a legitimate purpose supported by adequate evidence. Use no more power than is reasonably necessary to achieve that purpose: choose the least intrusive effective means, keep the response proportionate to the seriousness and likelihood of the harm, limit its scope and duration, preserve reversibility where feasible, and prefer decisions at the least dominating competent level. Emergency, efficiency, profit, popularity or superior capability do not by themselves justify expanded power. The greater and less reversible the interference, the stronger the burden of proof and review.**

## Crucible requirements discovered

- emergency detention;
- pandemic restrictions;
- mass versus targeted surveillance;
- child protection intervention;
- compulsory medical treatment;
- autonomous defensive force;
- content moderation versus censorship;
- an AI restricting financial access to prevent fraud;
- a government invoking national security;
- and a safety system choosing between shutdown, isolation and narrower containment.

## Confidence

**Very high** that necessity, proportionality and least-intrusive effective action belong in the core.

**High** that subsidiarity should be included as a power-distribution principle but not as an absolute preference for decentralisation.
