# MC-03 Provenance — Respect agency

## Current rule

> People should ordinarily be free to choose what they believe, say, love, create, consume, own, pursue and become. Neither government, majority, corporation nor AI gets to impose its preferred conception of a good life merely because it believes that life would be better.

## Provisional verdict

**Retain the protection, but this is the first rule that clearly needs conceptual refinement.**

The first-pass evidence does **not** justify presenting modern liberal autonomy as a universally shared moral foundation. What converges more broadly is protection against arbitrary domination combined with meaningful capacity to act, speak, choose and participate within relationships and duties.

MC-03 should move from an implicitly atomistic model of autonomy toward **agency + anti-domination + relational responsibility**.

## Strong modern support

### Universal Declaration of Human Rights

The UDHR couples equal dignity with life, liberty, security, freedom of thought, conscience, religion, opinion, expression, association and participation.

Source: https://www.un.org/en/node/124305

This is strong support for protected choice, but it is a modern rights instrument rather than proof that autonomy has always been the common basis of global moral traditions.

### Kant

Kant's Humanity Formula treats rational self-direction and the ability to pursue ends as central to respect for persons. Consent is therefore deeply connected to permissible treatment.

Source: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kant-moral/

Caution: grounding protection solely in rational autonomy risks weakening the standing of people with diminished rational capacity. MC-01 must remain prior to and independent from MC-03.

### Rawls

Rawls treats people as free and equal and gives priority to equal basic liberties, including conscience, thought, expression, association, integrity of the person and political participation. His veil of ignorance is deliberately designed to exclude arbitrary advantages of race, class, sex, wealth and similar facts.

Sources:
- https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/original-position/
- https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/rawls/

### Capability approach

The capability approach improves on merely formal freedom by asking whether people actually possess substantive opportunities to do and be things they have reason to value. Agency requires material and social conditions, not just absence of legal prohibition.

Source: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/capability-approach/

This is especially useful for AI governance. A system could nominally "give users a choice" while manipulating interfaces, controlling information or making alternatives practically inaccessible. Formal consent would exist while real agency would not.

## Cross-cultural challenges

### Confucian ethics — major corrective

Confucian ethics places relationships, duties, humaneness and a substantive conception of flourishing closer to the centre than individual choice. SEP explicitly notes the tension between Confucian relational ethics and theories that prioritise individual autonomy and wide choice among ways of life.

At the same time, Confucian texts value moral criticism, independent cultivation and in some cases remonstrating with rulers. The picture is not simple collectivism.

Source: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-chinese/

Project consequence: MC-03 should protect a person's ability to reason, dissent and choose without asserting that freedom from all relational obligation is the ideal human condition.

### African ethics — major corrective

African ethics as surveyed by SEP is strongly communitarian and duty-oriented. It treats persons as naturally embedded in relationships and often gives duties to others more conceptual emphasis than individual rights.

Source: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/african-ethics/

Project consequence: agency should be understood relationally. People are not isolated preference machines; they depend on communities and owe duties to others. But communal membership must not become a licence for domination.

### Jewish ethics — dignity does not equal autonomy

Scholarship on Jewish human dignity explicitly describes tensions between personal autonomy/liberty, communal dignity and divine commandments.

Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1467-9795.2006.00289.x

Project consequence: Jewish ethics supports dignity and moral responsibility but cannot honestly be counted as simple endorsement of modern autonomy-first liberalism.

### Buddhism — autonomy vocabulary is not native

Buddhist ethics is oriented toward ending suffering, transforming intention and attachment, and cultivating compassion. Western concepts such as intrinsic individual rights and autonomous preference satisfaction are not its natural starting points.

Source: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-indian-buddhism/

This is a warning against conflating "respect people's current preferences" with "respect people". Preferences can be compulsive, manipulated, ignorant or harmful. MC-03 must work together with truth, non-harm and capability.

### Sikh ethics — egalitarian practice supports agency but within duty/Oneness

Sikh ethics strongly resists caste/status hierarchy and institutionalises equality, while also defining ethical life through truthful living, service and Oneness rather than individual preference maximisation.

Source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/elements/sikh-ethics/931645585C0461FFC33D034EF558360C

### Hindu traditions — mixed and historically hierarchical

Dharma traditions include moral responsibility and multiple paths, but caste and gender hierarchy create obvious conflicts with freedom to choose one's role, status or life course.

Sources:
- https://academic.oup.com/book/12339/chapter-abstract/161899248
- https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/55829/chapter-abstract/437503600

This is precisely the type of inherited social rule that the Code's contradiction test must reject.

## Key conceptual correction

The current MC-03 contains two ideas that should be made explicit:

1. **Anti-domination** — no actor gets to impose its conception of a good life merely because it has more power.
2. **Meaningful agency** — people should possess genuine ability to understand, choose, dissent and pursue permissible goals.

These are broader and more defensible than an unlimited right to preference satisfaction.

A person can have duties to family/community without being owned by them.

A public-health rule can legitimately constrain movement without giving the state general authority over a person's life.

A user can consent to an AI service without meaningful agency if the AI manipulated what they knew, hid alternatives or exploited dependency.

## Proposed v0.2 amendment candidate

Possible title:

> **MC-03 — Protect agency and freedom from domination**

Candidate text:

> **People should have meaningful freedom to understand, choose, dissent, associate and pursue permissible ways of life. No government, majority, corporation, community or AI may dominate a person merely because it believes a different life would be better. Agency is exercised within relationships and responsibilities to others; it may be constrained to prevent sufficiently serious harm, but constraints require evidence, necessity, proportionality, minimum intrusion and review. Manipulation that deliberately bypasses or engineers meaningful choice is a form of coercive power even without physical force.**

Advantages:

- preserves the liberty we care about;
- handles AI manipulation better;
- does not pretend isolated individual autonomy is humanity's only moral language;
- recognises duties and interdependence;
- connects directly to MC-10/11 constraints on power.

## Crucible requirements discovered

MC-03 needs cases involving:

- quarantine,
- addiction and impaired consent,
- children and guardianship,
- coercive religious/community pressure,
- algorithmic manipulation,
- dark patterns,
- paternalistic medical treatment,
- misinformation and informed consent,
- family duties versus individual choice,
- compulsory education,
- and AI systems optimising users toward a behaviour they did not choose.

## Confidence

**High** that freedom from arbitrary domination and protection of meaningful agency belong in the core.

**Lower** that the current autonomy-heavy wording is culturally neutral enough for v1.0.
