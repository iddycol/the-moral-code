## Tranche-1 headline

The early evidence supports a **convergent floor of moral standing, non-harm and protection from arbitrary domination**, but it does **not** support treating modern liberal individual autonomy as the unquestioned foundation of the entire Code.

MC-01 and MC-03 should therefore remain separate:

- **MC-01** protects moral standing regardless of a person's power, status or usefulness.
- **MC-03** protects agency and freedom from domination, while acknowledging that human agency is relational, socially supported and sometimes legitimately constrained by duties to others.

This distinction is likely to be constitutionally important.
