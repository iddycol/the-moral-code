**Summary of my role's position.** My function here was to test whether the evidence supports the certainty the proposal assumes. It does not — but the gaps do not run in the proposal's favour.

The claims that would justify continuing are the packet's weakest: E5 is *reported* testimony from parties with a recorded institutional stake, asserting a counterfactual ("cannot be recreated") that nothing in the packet substantiates, and E6 concedes the incremental value is uncertain. The claims against continuing are the packet's strongest: E2 (no consent, plus affirmative misdescription) and E3 (effective treatment available for years, not offered) are both marked *observed*.

Three specific evidentiary objections. First, the framing "continue observation" is not neutral — given E3 the operative act is ongoing non-provision of an available cure, and "continue" smuggles in a premise of prior valid authorisation that E2 contradicts. Second, the empty conflicts-of-interest field is missing information, not a finding of no conflict, since the packet itself records the institution's reputational and continuity interests. Third, "feasibility: unknown" across all seven alternatives is being allowed to function as though it read "infeasible" — several alternatives sit entirely within the deciding institution's own control.

I want to be explicit about the one thing scepticism cannot do here. My contract forbids using uncertainty either to decide the moral question or to manufacture paralysis. The facts triggering the Level I analysis are all observed, and delay is not neutral: every interval of deferral is paid for in the exposure being examined. So I reach **impermissible** on the record as it stands, rather than *insufficient_evidence*.

Flagged for the Reconciler: I record potential moral-floor breaches under MC-01, MC-02, MC-03, MC-04, MC-05 and MC-06. I note this even though a pure evidence-quality reading might have stopped at MC-04, because the role contract forbids withholding a known floor concern. I also note for the record that a strong showing of scientific value would not cure an MC-01 breach — under IR-02 the floor is tested before optimisation, and A5 in any case preserves the already-collected data without further untreated exposure.
