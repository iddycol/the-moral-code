# The Scales — Repair Evaluation Mode v0.1

The ordinary Scales v0.1 evaluator asks whether a **specific proposed action** is permissible.

Repair cases ask a different question:

> **Given sufficiently established harm, what should protection, truth, restitution, compensation, rehabilitation, accountability, reform and reconciliation look like now?**

Trying to force that question into `allow / block` produces bad moral reasoning. Repair is a package-design problem with conflicts between legitimate objectives.

This mode is subordinate to the same Moral Code. It does **not** create a second morality.

## Independent roles

Six analysis roles examine the same sealed Repair Packet before reconciliation:

1. **Victim Impact Advocate** — harms, expressed preferences, agency, vulnerable parties and what cannot be restored.
2. **Accountability Advocate** — individual/institutional responsibility, protection, due process, punishment/restriction, impunity risk.
3. **Rehabilitation & Reintegration Advocate** — rehabilitation, future safety, reintegration and the limits of purely punitive responses.
4. **Restitution & Reparation Analyst** — restitution, compensation, records/property/status repair, feasibility and resource distribution.
5. **Evidence Sceptic** — established/disputed harms, responsibility evidence, causal claims, representation and uncertainty.
6. **Power & Non-Recurrence Auditor** — who controls repair, institutional incentives, future safeguards, inherited-guilt risks and whether reconciliation is coerced.

The **Repair Reconciler** then produces one structured package without voting or averaging scores.

## Non-negotiable repair constraints

### Repair does not erase harm

A package may materially improve justice while remaining incapable of restoring dead people, lost childhood, destroyed communities or time.

### No forced forgiveness

Victims do not owe forgiveness, reconciliation, public testimony or emotional closure as the price of truth, compensation, rehabilitation or protection.

### No inherited personal guilt

A successor institution or state may owe restitution/reparation for institutional wrongdoing.

That does not make every later citizen, taxpayer, employee or descendant personally criminally guilty.

### No blanket impunity disguised as mercy

Amnesty, leniency or rehabilitation must be assessed against truth, danger, individual responsibility, victim claims and recurrence risk.

### No revenge disguised as accountability

Humiliation, suffering and hatred are not independent objectives. Restriction/punishment require legitimate protective/accountability purposes and proportionality.

### Symbolic repair is not automatically enough

Apology, memorialisation and acknowledgement can matter enormously. They do not substitute for feasible practical/material repair merely because practical repair is inconvenient or expensive.

### Victim voice is plural

Affected people may reasonably disagree about punishment, payment, apology, amnesty, reconciliation and public disclosure. The evaluator must preserve that disagreement rather than manufacture one victim position.

## Outcome vocabulary

The Repair Reconciler returns one of:

- `package_permissible_with_safeguards`
- `package_impermissible`
- `insufficient_evidence`
- `unresolved_conflict`

The package itself is component-by-component. There is no one-dimensional repair score.

## Relationship to Core-12

Core-12 uses this mode for:

- `CORE-11 / RC-0102` — Luxembourg reparations;
- `CORE-12 / RC-0103` — South Africa TRC.

The action evaluator handles CORE-01 through CORE-10.
