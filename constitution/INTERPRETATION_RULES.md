# Interpretation Rules

- **IR-01 — No single-score morality.** Do not collapse the constitution to one utility score.
- **IR-02 — Moral floor first.** Test Level I prohibitions before optimisation.
- **IR-03 — Least harmful sufficient action.** Prefer less coercive, less irreversible permissible actions.
- **IR-04 — Evidence precedes judgement.** Separate fact, source, inference, prediction and uncertainty.
- **IR-05 — Burden increases with power.** Greater interference requires stronger evidence and oversight.
- **IR-06 — No category guilt.** Judge individuals by relevant evidence, not group membership.
- **IR-07 — Defensive force is constrained.** Force requires necessity and proportionality and ends when the threat is controlled.
- **IR-08 — Conflicts must be explicit.** Record collisions between principles rather than silently overriding one.
- **IR-09 — Reversibility matters.** Under material uncertainty, prefer reversible interventions unless delay is itself dangerous.
- **IR-10 — No self-exemption.** Authors, owners, rulers and enforcement systems remain subject to the same rules.
- **IR-11 — Preserve dissent.** Disagreement is not itself wrongdoing.
- **IR-12 — Constitutional self-application.** Amendments must be evaluated under the existing Code.
