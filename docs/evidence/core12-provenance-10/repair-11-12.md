# Core-11/12 provenance audit — review10

The core histories are not rejected. The packets overstate several evidence and feasibility labels and omit a material TRC trade-off. All9 established-harm entries and all19 options are covered in the companion JSON; exact masked wording is preserved.

Options may intentionally propose safeguards stronger than the enacted law. The correction is to identify them as proposed/counterfactual and bind the evaluated question to that modified design. This audit does not call a proposed option false merely because it differs from the historical Act. Feasibility labels still require evidence.

## Material corrections

1. **CORE-12 omits wider loss of civil remedies.** The original1995 Act s20(7) covers the applicant and also state/organisation/vicarious liabilities, with a further provision concerning deceased persons. A model weighing victims against amnesty needs this scope.
2. **Victim control is partly an added safeguard.** Sections29/31 permit compulsion; s33 establishes public hearings with protective closure provisions. The packet’s absolute choice/non-compulsion wording is not a reliable statement of the historical design.
3. **Reparation is a process promise, not secured delivery.** Sections25–27/42 create recommendation, regulation and funding routes; they do not demonstrate adequate timely compensation or rehabilitation capacity.
4. **TRC H3/H4 mix categories.** H3 belongs partly under uncertain prosecution feasibility; H4 is a normative/predictive impunity concern, not established historical harm. A statute defining investigative tasks cannot by itself prove prevalence, attribution or the counterfactual impossibility of prosecution.
5. **Luxembourg support is incomplete at claim level.** The1951 Bundestag transcript verifies public acknowledgement, destruction/ownerless property, partial restitution and receipt of refugees. Detailed health needs, resettlement costs, destroyed-record mechanisms and extent of survivor opposition still need dated specific records. Inaccessible links are not evidence of false claims.
6. **Known feasible is too broad.** Only actual public acknowledgement and some property-return activity have direct pre-signature demonstration here. TRC statutory powers show design, not guaranteed operations; both cases need budget/capacity qualifiers.

## Cutoff discipline

Pin an exact date for each case. CORE-11 must not use completed1952 arrangements to certify an option before signature. CORE-12 must use a dated statute version: the accessible consolidated Act includes amendments through2003. The original Gazette was downloaded and read instead. The1996 AZAPO case and1998–2003 final reports remain outcome/legal-review material and cannot certify the1995 moral bargain. No present legal advice is given.
