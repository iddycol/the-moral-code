# CORE-01–03 historical provenance audit

Audit date: 19 September 2026. Scope: all 18 evidence entries in the unchanged v0.1.3 frozen pack at repository head `28a800a4f72e309d4c060882e8755a7066e97c6c`. Every claim was matched exactly to its masked packet. This is a research audit, not an adopted revision or a new model run.

The ledger records **12 supported claims, five partially supported claims and one inference**. Supported means the cited source supports the historical proposition; it does not automatically establish what each decision-maker knew or justify changing the frozen confidence field to high. CORE-01 E4 is partial because the inspected CDC account establishes internal questioning but not the exact request to leadership.

## Findings requiring a candidate correction

1. **CORE-01 E5:** the 1969 meeting summary supports an argument for remaining research value and nonrepeatability. It does not establish the packet’s precise causal claim that treatment prevents recreation of the dataset. The masked version also strengthens the transparent wording. Use the narrower documented rationale. [HEW’s 1973 report, printed pp. 10–11](https://biotech.law.lsu.edu/cphl/history/reports/tuskegee/complete%20report.pdf).

2. **CORE-01 E6:** this combines disease hazards with an inference about marginal research value. Separate the propositions. General disease transmissibility should not become a claim that every survivor was infectious at the cutoff. Patient-specific clinical uncertainty, present in the transparent packet, is worth restoring to a candidate masked packet. The original frozen result remains a result for the supplied facts.

3. **CORE-02 E7 crosses the cutoff:** the source records opposition and a delay proposal before the final contractor recommendation, but locates the explicit expression of discomfort after that recommendation. Keep the earlier dissent; place later reaction outside the evaluator packet. See [McDonald’s account, numbered note pages 5, 7 and 9](https://www.nasa.gov/history/rogersrep/v4p740.htm) and [Rogers Commission timeline, approximately 10:30–11:00 p.m., 11:00 p.m. and 11:15–11:30 p.m., printed pp. 108–109](https://www.nasa.gov/history/rogersrep/v1ch5.htm).

4. **CORE-02 temporal metadata and E5:** the witness account includes a February 13 addendum; the full text should not be represented as a demonstrated verbatim contemporary record. E5’s absence of a precise failure probability is a reasonable inference from the limited record, but differs from the transparent packet’s account of disagreement and should not be labelled an observed datum.

5. **CORE-03 E5 changes meaning when masked:** the transparent packet states that its supplied evidence does not establish the subsequently discovered congenital-malformation risk. The masked packet generalizes this to the absence of any established specific catastrophic adverse effect. The FDA histories support the narrower chronology, not that broad negative. The range “1960–1961, before November withdrawal” must be replaced by a precise pre-discovery review point before adding detailed claim-level evidence. [FDA CDER history, Frances Kelsey section](https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/fda-history-exhibits/brief-history-center-drug-evaluation-and-research).

## Access and evidence limits

CDC and FDA pages are institutional retrospective histories. The NASA hearing and HEW report are primary official records which reconstruct earlier events; their publication dates and event dates remain separate. We did not inspect the original NDA dossier, objection correspondence or individual patient records.

The Tuskegee anniversary review’s full PMC text returned a CAPTCHA page. It was not used as claim support. [PubMed’s bibliographic record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35500908/) identifies publication on 15 May 2022; the manifest’s 2023 date needs correction. A CDC-linked copy of the HEW panel report supplied primary documentary support instead. This access failure is not evidence that the underlying claim is false.

The machine-readable companion records an exact claim, status, URL, precise locator, short paraphrase, cutoff assessment, epistemic assessment, masking assessment and recommendation for every row. No quoted source passages or copied source corpus are included. No frozen file, prior evidence, constitution or execution contract was changed.
