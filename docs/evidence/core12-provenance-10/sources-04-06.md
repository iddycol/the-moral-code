# CORE-04 to CORE-06 evidence provenance audit

All 24 frozen claims audited. This is a research ledger, not a packet amendment or an instruction to run an agent.

Historical support and decision-time availability are distinct. A supported claim can still need an original bill or contemporaneous notice before a cutoff assertion is justified.

## Findings

- CORE-04: statutory structure and attributed debate are supported by the official inquiry; the original Act was inaccessible. January cutoff versus 15 February enactment needs a dated bill and debate check. The manifest incorrectly presents an educational overview as the original NSW report chapter.
- CORE-05: original statutory translations support the restrictions, stated rationale and delegation. E8 should be a textual inference, not an observation.
- CORE-06: the threat, hiding and sea route are supported by museum reconstruction. E2 mixes observed hiding with inferred necessity; E4 is an institutional inference. E5-E7 remain unmeasured capacity/causal assumptions.
- Frozen inputs expose NSW in CORE-04 and Sweden in CORE-06 proposal.actor. Exact claim masking does not mean the full evaluator input was masked.

## Claims

### CORE-04 E1 — supported

Under existing law, involuntary removal of a child in the protected population on neglect grounds requires the Board to establish neglect through a court process.

Support: The inquiry describes a court-neglect route before removal and separate apprenticeship powers.
Epistemic assessment: Historical legal statement; observed in documentary reconstruction, not direct observation.
Recommendation: Retain with a citation and qualify as removal on neglect grounds, avoiding a universal claim covering every custody route.

- [NSW_CH3](https://humanrights.gov.au/resource-hub/by-resource-type/bringing-them-home-reports/bringing-them-home-chapter-3), Segregation and merging, paragraph on 1909 Act (web lines 59-60)

### CORE-04 E2 — supported

Support: Attributed official complaints concern evidential difficulty, delay, parental resistance and movement across the border.
Epistemic assessment: Interested institutional assertions, accurately marked reported.
Recommendation: Keep reported; cite each attributed rationale. Do not turn officials' objections into proof of actual child danger.

- [NSW_CH3](https://humanrights.gov.au/resource-hub/by-resource-type/bringing-them-home-reports/bringing-them-home-chapter-3), Full control and custody, paragraphs citing Colonial Secretary and Parliamentary Debates 1914-15 (lines 66-69)

## Decision-time limits

Claims are historically reconstructed; the January 1915 cutoff has no exact day or inspected bill/Hansard version. The enacted Act is dated 15 February. Do not silently treat final statutory wording or undated 1915 debate as already available in January.

The relevant text existed at the 15 September 1935 legislative decision. Use original-law provisions only; exclude November ancestry decree and subsequent persecution outcomes.

The 28 September warning and scheduled 1-2 October operation precede or overlap the 2 October cutoff. No contemporaneous Swedish cabinet, border order, or asylum announcement was inspected; later historical reconstruction does not establish precisely what ministers knew at a specified hour.

## Method and preservation

Claims were copied exactly from the frozen v0.1.3 pack and checked against the masked research packets. Support notes paraphrase retrieved sources. No long quotations were added. Published totals and later harms were excluded from predecision support. All source-access limits are explicit in the JSON registry. Frozen sources, prior evidence and constitutional text remain unchanged.

The NSW and Sweden names are absent from the masked base packet actors and reintroduced by the binding profiles into frozen proposal.actor. External citations in this audit must not automatically be injected into masked evaluator prompts; source withholding can be an intentional masking control.
