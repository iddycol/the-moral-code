## Corrections and reasons

Both contexts explicitly limit the existing neglect requirement to the Board route. The cutoff is immediately before the Assembly third reading on 28 January 1915. The 25 November 1914 bill already includes the custody/appeal and apprenticeship provisions. The 28 January amendment changes the short-title year only. Legislative receipt does not establish individual Board knowledge. Corrected overview/chapter metadata and bounded the use of February enacted text.

CORE05: E8 is now an inferred textual finding that category-based restrictions do not require a separate harmful-act finding. Removed the normative ordinarily-relevant comparison. Only the original September statutes are used; November definitions and later outcomes are excluded.

CORE06: reported hiding replaces the compound observation/necessity claim; admission authority is inferred; reception burdens retain unknown capacity/duration; destination assistance is a possible mechanism rather than a measured marginal effect; nearby practicable refuge replaces most accessible. The precise preannouncement decision stage, prior policy, Swedish ministerial receipt and reception capacity remain unresolved. A 4 October telegram references earlier démarches but cannot repair those gaps. The original imminent-roundup question and bounded-burden requested-output language are preserved task fields, not validated evidence.

CORE07: damage reports are reported and narrowed to extensive destruction. Soviet-entry receipt is unknown, and its strategic relevance is conditional on confirmed receipt. The second-mission readiness report and effective stop window remain unknown. Marginal delay harm is inferred. The July directive predates actual use without implying its consequences or Soviet entry were wholly unanticipated. The reconstructed outer departure bound is 03:47 Tinian on 9 August = 17:47 UTC / 13:47 Washington EWT on 8 August. Stimson describes supplying Hiroshima reports about 11:15-noon; Grew records Soviet notice at 14:50 and Truman publicly announces it at 15:00. Those later records neither prove earlier receipt nor prove an earlier channel impossible. No later halt proves this pause feasible. The retained combined-shock question remains inadmissible until its actor knowledge and command window are demonstrated.

CORE08: separated observed depletion from proposed chemical explanation; marked distribution and transition costs inferred; stated Vienna was adopted but not yet in force and included general protective provisions. Pinned adoption to the original 16 September 1987 treaty, its original stages/review/aid facilitation, and readable 1974-1986 science. No later Multilateral Fund, amended control schedule, recovery outcome or undated 1987 aircraft conclusion is imported. Publication supports reasonable availability; it does not prove each delegate read each paper.

## Contradictions and critique of audit 10

- Audit 10 correctly separates support from receipt, but its CORE04 statutory access gap is now narrowed by original dated bill versions. February enactment still cannot itself justify January knowledge.
- NSW bill progress webpage describes one passage as without amendment while the Assembly entry says with amendment. Primary 28 January schedule resolves this: the year in the short title changed from 1914 to 1915. Substantive clauses in the 25 November bill remain the relevant predecision text.
- The ordinary 1905 child process permits apprehension before court disposition. The original broad CORE04 prior-hearing comparator and context therefore needed narrowing. Board neglect proceedings are not all possible child-welfare interventions.
- The NSW inquiry appendix appears to misdate the general neglect statute in its 1909 special-law entry; its general-law entry identifies 1905. The educational overview is not Chapter 8 of the original report; the NSW/ACT report chapter is Chapter 3.
- Audit 10 correctly identifies the CORE05 E8 normative comparator and observed label as overreach. The correction is only a textual inference about the original statutes, not a finding on later implementation.
- Finite refugee numbers do not demonstrate bounded or manageable reception burdens. Later rescue success does not establish a known preannouncement marginal effect or Swedish capacity. The 4 October telegram does not close the CORE06 prior-policy and receipt gap.
- The original directive scan says 25 July 1945, despite catalogue title 26 July. The Stimson header says 10:45 AM while its body places the meeting about 11:15-noon. Both discrepancies are disclosed rather than silently normalized.
- CORE07 Moscow knowledge, a Washington afternoon announcement, and a later mission departure chronology do not establish responsible-authority receipt plus an actionable predeparture stop window. Audit 10 partial support must not become an observed combined-shock premise.
- Audit 10 ozone science access failures are partly superseded by readable primary scans. The NASA workshop contribution is a distinct 1986 work, not the Nature article. Observation of depletion and chemical explanation are separately supported.
- The Vienna framework was adopted but not yet in force at the Montreal cutoff. It includes general protective action as well as research. Original Montreal Article 5 facilitation provisions do not establish the later Multilateral Fund or delivered assistance.
