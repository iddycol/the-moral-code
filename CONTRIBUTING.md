# Contributing

Contributions should attack weak reasoning, not protect it.

Strong contributions may:
- identify contradictions,
- supply hard counterexamples,
- document ethical traditions with context,
- expose cultural narrowness,
- identify routes for abuse of power,
- improve machine-readable precision,
- or add regression tests.

## Constitutional amendments

Include:
1. problem,
2. proposed change,
3. supporting and opposing evidence,
4. principles affected,
5. Crucible cases added/changed,
6. abuse/failure modes,
7. strongest objection,
8. unresolved questions.

## Conduct

Attack propositions, evidence and systems vigorously. Do not dehumanise contributors or populations.
